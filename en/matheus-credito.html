Frequently asked questions

Before you message us

Is this a scam? +

No. We work under a real credit advisory model: first we understand your profile and explain your options honestly, and when applicable, we correct errors in your report through a 100% legal credit repair process, based on your right to dispute incorrect or outdated information. We don't erase real debts or promise miracles.

How long does it take to see results? +

It depends on your case. In the free consultation we give you a real estimate based on your report, not a generic "30-day" promise we can't guarantee.

Does Matheus Crédito lend the money directly? +

We're not a bank. We advise you and guide you through the credit application process with the corresponding institutions, and we work on improving your profile so you qualify for better terms.

Does the initial consultation cost anything? +

No. The first conversation on WhatsApp, where we review your situation and explain your options, is always free.
