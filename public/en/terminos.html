6. Affiliate program

The affiliate program lets a business open its service so other people can recommend it with their own link and earn a commission on the sales they bring.

Who can be an affiliate. Anyone 18 or older with a Sevrii account. Affiliates act on their own, as independent contractors: they are not employees, agents, partners or representatives of Sevrii or of the business they recommend, and they cannot make promises or sign agreements on their behalf.

For businesses. You can open your service to affiliates if your page is published and Payments is active. You choose the commission, from 5% to 50% of the amount the customer pays. The commission is deducted automatically from each payment attributed to an affiliate, together with the Payments fee (section 5), and Sevrii delivers it to the affiliate. You can change the percentage or close the program at any time; changes apply to new sales. For monthly access sold by an affiliate, the commission you agreed to keeps being deducted on each renewal while the customer keeps paying. You remain the seller and are responsible for your service, your customers and your refunds.

Which sales count. Only payments made through Sevrii (payment links and AI agent access) by a customer who arrived through the affiliate's link within the 30 days before the payment. The visit is stored in the customer's browser; if they come through several affiliates' links, the last one counts. Payments made outside Sevrii, purchases by the affiliate or their own business, and sales involving false data or fraud do not count. If the customer clears their cookies or pays from another device, the sale may not be attributed; Sevrii's records govern, except for an obvious error.

When and how it is paid. Each commission is held for 30 days after the payment. If during that time the payment is refunded or disputed, the commission is voided. After that, Sevrii transfers it to the affiliate's Stripe account, and Stripe deposits it in their bank. To get paid, the affiliate must create that account from Sevrii, accept the Stripe Connected Account Agreement and provide the information Stripe requests, including tax information. Until they connect it, their commissions are kept; if they do not connect it within 12 months, we may treat them as forfeited. If a payment is refunded or disputed after the commission was paid, we may deduct it from future commissions.

Taxes. Each affiliate is responsible for reporting and paying taxes on their commissions. Stripe may request tax information and issue the forms the law requires (for example, Form 1099).

Rules for affiliates. You must clearly disclose that you earn a commission when you recommend a service, as the FTC guides require. You may not: make false claims or promise results the business does not promise; post fake reviews or testimonials; send spam by email, text message or social media, or contact people without their consent; impersonate the business or Sevrii; use the business's brand in paid ads without its permission; generate fake visits, clicks or purchases; or buy through your own link.

Data. The business sees each affiliate's code and their sales, not their email. The affiliate sees their visits, sales and commissions, not the customers' data.

Breaches and changes. We may hold or void commissions, remove services from the program and suspend affiliates who break these rules or who we see at risk of fraud.
