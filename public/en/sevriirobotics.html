Two ways to earn

Your robot, your business

We imagine a future where robots don't only belong to big companies. Anyone could earn money with them in two ways.

01

Own your robot

You have your own Sevrii robot and put it to work. Sevrii finds it cleaning, moving, installation and other service jobs, and you get paid for each one.

  • Your robot is listed on the Sevrii marketplace
  • Sevrii finds customers and manages the schedule
  • You get paid for every job it does
02

Work through the robot

If you know a trade, you offer your service from wherever you are: you connect and control a robot remotely, in real time, right where the customer needs it.

  • You control the robot remotely
  • Serve customers in other cities without traveling
  • Your skills and your hands, through the robot

Can I buy or hire a Sevrii robot today?+

No. Sevrii Robotics is our vision for the future and is not an available product or service yet. The images on this page are concepts.

What can I do with Sevrii today?+

Use the software: the Brain builds your strategy, Store creates your sales page and Marketing prepares your campaigns. You can start free.

Will robots replace professionals?+

That's not the idea. We imagine robots that help professionals do more and do it better, with the person always in charge.

Will I be able to own a robot and make money with it?+

That's the vision: anyone could own a robot and put it to work on the Sevrii marketplace, or offer their service by controlling a robot remotely. It isn't available yet.
