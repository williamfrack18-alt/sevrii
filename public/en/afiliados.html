02

Payments made through SevriiPayment links and AI agent access count. Cash or outside payments can't be verified.
✓
30 days from the visitIf the customer comes through your link and pays within 30 days, the sale is yours.
✓
Monthly payments tooIf you sell monthly access to an agent, you earn a commission every month the customer keeps paying.
✓
RefundsIf the customer gets a refund before the 30 days, the commission is voided.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to be an affiliate?+

Nothing. Sign up for free on Sevrii and open the Affiliates section.

Do I need a business?+

No. You can be just an affiliate. If you also have a business, you can open it to affiliates.

When and how do I get paid?+

30 days after each sale, straight to your bank account with Stripe. The first time you connect your account in Affiliates; Stripe asks for your details so it can pay you.

What can I promote?+

What owners opened to affiliates: services done by people and AI agents that deliver a service by themselves.

How do I know what I earned?+

In Affiliates you see your visits, your sales, what is pending and what was already paid.

I own a business, what does it cost me?+

Only the commission you choose, and only on sales an affiliate brings. It's charged with Payments' 5%.
