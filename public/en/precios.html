Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You cancel from the Plan section of your account and keep what you paid for until the end of the period.

What happens if I reach a limit?

We let you know in your dashboard. You can wait for next month or switch to a plan with more usage.

Do prices include taxes?

Prices are in US dollars and applicable taxes may be added.
