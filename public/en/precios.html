Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You cancel from the Plan section of your account and keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for (3, 6 or 12 months).

What happens if I reach a limit?

We let you know in your dashboard. You can wait for next month; usage renews every month, whether you pay quarterly, half-yearly or yearly.

Do prices include taxes?

Prices are in US dollars and applicable taxes may be added.
